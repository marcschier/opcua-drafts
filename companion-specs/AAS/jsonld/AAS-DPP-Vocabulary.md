# Digital Product Passport and Battery Passport — identifier mapping

**Status — working draft.** This document defines how the semantic identifiers of the IDTA Digital
Product Passport and Digital Battery Passport submodel templates are used as RDF resources, and how
the access tiers of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 relate to the disclosure model of the OPC UA AAS
companion specification. It is not published by, endorsed by, or submitted to the Industrial Digital
Twin Association.

## 1 What this document does, and what it does not

It does **not** mint a term per passport attribute. The templates already carry semantic identifiers
on every element, and a parallel vocabulary would add a second name for each without adding meaning.

It defines three things:

- how an identifier that cannot be used as an RDF IRI as written is given one (clause 3);
- which mapping predicate is used, and why it is not `owl:equivalentProperty` (clause 4);
- how the access tiers of the Batteries Regulation map onto `DisclosureTier` (clause 5).

The mapping set itself is data, not prose: `dpp/mappings.sssom.tsv`, generated by
`tools/dpp_map.py` and regenerable from the published templates.

Two figures decide the design.

**64.9% of distinct identifiers are already legal IRIs.** A URN is an IRI whether or not it
dereferences, so `urn:samm:io.admin-shell.idta.batterypass…` needs no construction, no mapping row
and no minted term. Only 185 of 527 need anything done to them.

**ECLASS is a minority scheme, and IEC CDD is marginal.** The largest single scheme is SAMM URNs.
Any design premised on ECLASS carrying the bulk of the work would have been built on a quarter of
the data.

Identifiers are attached to `semanticId` (1 588 occurrences), `supplementalSemanticIds` (616),
`valueId` (178) and `unitId` (68), and are carried by `Property` (1 312),
`SubmodelElementCollection` (412), `SubmodelElementList` (254), `DataSpecificationIec61360` (244)
and `MultiLanguageProperty` (138).

## 3 Identifier construction (normative)

For a semantic identifier taken from a template, an implementation **MUST** apply the first rule
that matches.

1. **Already an IRI.** Where the identifier is an absolute IRI containing at most one `#`, it
   **MUST** be used unchanged.
2. **ECLASS IRDI.** Where the identifier begins `0173-1#`, the IRI **MUST** be

   ```text
   https://rdf.eclass.eu/resource/<identifier with every # replaced by _>
   ```

   as published in *ECLASS Serialization as RDF, Part 1*.
3. **Otherwise.** The IRI **MUST** be

   ```text
   https://w3id.org/aas-dpp/id/<hex>
   ```

   where `<hex>` is the lower-case hexadecimal SHA-256 of the UTF-8 encoding of the identifier.

Rule 3 covers IEC CDD IRDIs and the remaining IRDI-shaped identifiers. IEC CDD publishes no
canonical dereferenceable IRI form; an implementation **MUST NOT** invent one under `cdd.iec.ch`.

An implementation **MUST** retain the identifier as written, in `aas:Key/value`, in every case. The
constructions of rules 2 and 3 are one way and **MUST NOT** be inverted.

An implementation **MUST** trim leading and trailing whitespace from an identifier before applying
these rules, and **SHOULD** report having done so. Two identifiers in the published templates carry
surrounding whitespace; untrimmed, they match no scheme and would be given a hash IRI in place of
the ECLASS IRI they have.

Applied to the current templates:

| | |
|---|---|
| used unchanged (rule 1) | 342 |
| ECLASS construction (rule 2) | 136 |
| hash construction (rule 3) | 49 |
| **usable as an RDF IRI after construction** | **527 of 527 (100%)** |
| of which dereferenceable today | 198 of 527 (37.6%) |

Most of the identifiers used unchanged are URNs, which are IRIs but do not dereference. The
construction makes every identifier usable as an RDF resource; it does not make most of them
retrievable, and this document does not claim otherwise.

## 4 Mapping predicate (normative)

A mapping row **MUST** use a SKOS predicate. It **MUST NOT** use `owl:equivalentProperty` or
`owl:sameAs`.

An AAS semantic identifier key is a `GlobalReference` in 1 504 of the 1 588 `semanticId`
occurrences, and in every `supplementalSemanticIds`, `valueId` and `unitId` occurrence. A
`GlobalReference` names a concept in an external dictionary. It is not an RDF property, and it is
not the element that carries it, so asserting property equivalence would be a category error even
where the concept and a property happen to correspond.

- Where every occurrence of an identifier is a `GlobalReference`, the predicate **MUST** be
  `skos:exactMatch`: the two IRIs are two names for one concept, which is what the construction
  guarantees.
- Otherwise the predicate **MUST** be `skos:closeMatch`.

A mapping row **MUST** carry its provenance: the source repository and version, the scheme, the
construction applied, whether the result dereferences, and a confidence. Rows produced by rules 1
and 2 carry confidence `1.0`, because the construction is published or trivial. Rows produced by
rule 3 carry `0.5`, because the IRI is stable but denotes nothing a consumer can retrieve.

## 5 Access tiers (normative)

Annex XIII of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 divides battery passport data into content available to the
public, content available to persons with a legitimate interest and the Commission, and content
available to notified bodies and market surveillance authorities. EN 18239 specifies requirements
for implementing tiered access for product passports generally.

Clause 9.7 of the OPC UA AAS companion specification defines two information disclosure tiers,
`Public` and `Controlled`, and states that a tier classifies the information rather than the caller.
The regulatory classes map onto them by deciding, per class, whether the content is readable without
authentication:

| Regulatory class | `DisclosureTier` |
|---|---|
| available to the public | `Public` |
| available to persons with a legitimate interest and the Commission | `Controlled` |
| available to notified bodies and market surveillance authorities | `Controlled` |

The two controlled classes are distinguished by the authorization a Consumer must present, not by
the tier. An implementation **MUST** advertise that authorization through the `Authorization`
attribute of clause 9.7 and **MUST NOT** rely on the tier alone to separate them.

Where a Server serves a passport through the registry and also materializes it, clause 9.10 applies:
the environment document served to a Session **MUST** contain only what that Session may read. An
implementation **MUST NOT** treat a `Public` tier on the submodel as permission to include
`Controlled` elements in an environment document.

An implementation **MUST NOT** infer from the absence of an element in a filtered document that the
element is absent from the passport, per clause 9.10.

## Annex A — The mapping set

`dpp/mappings.sssom.tsv`, in SSSOM column order. One row per identifier that needs a construction;
identifiers usable as written are not listed, and an implementation applies rule 1 to them.

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| `subject_id` | the identifier as written in the template, trimmed |
| `subject_label` | the metamodel classes that carry it |
| `predicate_id` | `skos:exactMatch` or `skos:closeMatch`, per clause 4 |
| `object_id` | the constructed IRI |
| `mapping_justification` | `identity`, `eclass-rdf-part1` or `aas-dpp-hash` |
| `subject_source`, `subject_source_version` | the template repository and branch |
| `object_source` | the identifier scheme |
| `confidence` | per clause 4 |
| `subject_type` | the key types the identifier appears as |
| `comment` | which members carry it, and whether the IRI dereferences |

## Annex B — Alignment to external vocabularies

This annex is informative, and deliberately short.

**CIRPASS-2 EUDPP** (`https://w3id.org/eudpp#`, Apache-2.0) is the closest thing to a horizontal EU
DPP ontology. It is a deliverable of a time-limited project rather than a standard, and the
CEN/CENELEC JTC 24 standards it feeds may diverge from it. It is therefore an alignment target
reached through `rdfs:seeAlso`, not a namespace this document builds on.

**QUDT** (CC BY 4.0) is the vocabulary for units. An AAS `unitId` is an IRDI and is carried through
clause 3 like any other identifier; a QUDT IRI is added alongside rather than in place of it.

**UNTP** is **GPL-3.0+**. It is not a dependency of this document and **MUST NOT** be made one; an
alignment may be published separately by anyone willing to accept that licence.

**ECLASS and IEC CDD content** is access-restricted. This document references identifiers and
constructs IRIs from them. It **MUST NOT** redistribute labels, definitions or value lists from
either dictionary.

## Annex C — Defects found in the published templates

This annex is informative.

- Two identifiers carry surrounding whitespace: `" 0173-1#05-AAA129#006"` and
  `"0173-1#07-ABZ789#003 "`. Both are ECLASS IRDIs that classify as unrecognised until trimmed.
- One identifier matches no known scheme after trimming.
